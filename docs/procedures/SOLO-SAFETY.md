# Solo work safety plan

Drafted for [#8](https://github.com/Braden2n/JoyRide/issues/8). It governs solo battery work until the builder decides otherwise.

## Principles

- No one will catch a mistake, so make mistakes harmless: low voltage, current limits, and fuses first.
- Work only when rested, fed, and unhurried. Stop when tired, sick, or distracted.
- Never work on live circuits. De-energize, lock out, and verify with a meter.
- Energize in stages, and check twice before each stage.
- Keep a way to get help: a charged phone within reach and a person who knows the plan.
- Stop at the first surprise. Do not troubleshoot a hot or smelling pack.
- Write the plan for the session before starting, and follow it.

## Periodic checks

## Procedures

### Before a session

1. Confirm you are rested and focused. If not, do not work on the battery.
2. Check in with a contact person before starting. Give them the start time, the task, and the expected end time. The contact is TBD.
3. Clear the bench. Remove metal jewelry. Keep a fire-safe surface under the pack.
4. Put the phone within reach, with signal.
5. Set the bench supply current limit before connecting anything.

### Working on the pack

1. Disconnect the load and charger. Open the contactor and the fuse or service disconnect.
2. Measure with a meter to confirm zero volts.
3. Use insulated tools. Work on one connection at a time.
4. Have a second look at every wiring change after a pause, before applying power.
5. Energize through a current limit first, then at full power.

### Charging and storage

1. Charge only in the designated area, in the container, and in sight.
2. Never leave a charging pack while you are away or asleep.
3. Store lithium cells only in the designated container.

### If something goes wrong

1. Open the E-stop or disconnect power if it is safe to reach.
2. Get away from the pack. Do not touch a hot, swelling, smoking, or venting pack.
3. Leave the area, and call emergency services for fire or injury.
4. Fight a fire only when it is small and you have a clear exit.
5. Record the event in the journal.

### After a session

1. Disconnect and secure the pack. Open the fuse or service disconnect.
2. Tell the check-in contact that you are done.
3. Log any near miss in the journal.
