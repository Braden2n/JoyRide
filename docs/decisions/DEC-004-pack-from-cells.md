# DEC-004: Battery pack assembled from cells

## Context

The kart needs a battery pack. The builder prefers to assemble it from cells, but the choice belongs to the [PACK cycle](../stages/STG-2-PACK.md).

## Options

| Option | Pros | Cons |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Li-ion cells assembled by the builder | Highest learning value | Most safety work (RSK-001) |
| LiFePO4 cells | TBD in the PACK cycle | TBD |
| Commercial pack with its own BMS | Least risk | Least learning |

## Decision

Proposed: assemble the pack from cells. Confirm or change at the STG-2-PACK spend checkpoint.
