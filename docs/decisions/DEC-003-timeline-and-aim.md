# DEC-003: About 18 months, aiming for Spark

## Context

The timeline could be closer to 12 or 24 months, and the aim could be any level on the [success ladder](../project/PROJECT.md#success-ladder).

## Options

| Option | Pros | Cons |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 12 months | Faster result | Tight at 6 to 10 hours a week |
| About 18 months | Fits the weekly time and the $150 monthly budget | Slower |
| 24 months | Most room | Higher risk of lost interest (RSK-009) |

## Decision

About 18 months, mainly for fun and learning. The current aim is Spark, and any higher level is a bonus.

## Consequences

The budget envelope is about $2,700. Spark covers STG-1, STG-2-DRV, and STG-2-PACK.
